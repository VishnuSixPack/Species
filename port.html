Port terminology

Understanding port facilities

Port
1 of 8
An entire harbour or maritime facility
A place where ships arrive, load and unload cargo, and receive services.
Terminal
2 of 8
A specialised area within a port
Handles one kind of cargo: containers, oil, LNG, passengers and so on.
Berth
3 of 8
The exact place where a ship ties up
Gives a ship its loading or unloading position inside a terminal or alongside a wharf.
Wharf
4 of 8
A long platform built along the shore
Ships moor directly alongside it to load and unload.
Pier
5 of 8
A structure that reaches out into the water
Ships can berth on one or both sides. Often used for passenger ferries.
Dock
6 of 8
A docking area or an enclosed basin
Used loosely for "where ships dock". Can be a dry dock for repair or a wet dock.
Jetty
7 of 8
A structure that reaches into the water
Usually built for wave protection. Sometimes used to moor smaller vessels.
Quay
8 of 8
A waterfront structure much like a wharf
The common term in Europe. Ships berth alongside it to handle cargo.
