Port Terminology Guide
Understanding facility types
🏭
Port
1 of 8
What it is
An entire harbour or maritime facility
Primary Purpose
A place where ships arrive, load/unload cargo, and receive services.
🚢
Terminal
2 of 8
What it is
A specialised area within a port
Primary Purpose
Handles a specific type of cargo — containers, oil, LNG, passengers, etc.
⚓
Berth
3 of 8
What it is
The exact location where a ship ties up
Primary Purpose
Assigns a ship to a loading/unloading position inside a terminal or alongside a wharf.
🏗
Wharf
4 of 8
What it is
A long platform built alongside the shore
Primary Purpose
Ships moor directly alongside it for loading and unloading.
🛳
Pier
5 of 8
What it is
A structure extending out into the water
Primary Purpose
Ships can berth on one or both sides, often used for passenger ferries.
🔧
Dock
6 of 8
What it is
A docking area or enclosed basin
Primary Purpose
Often used generally as "where ships dock." Can be dry dock (for repair) or wet dock.
🪝
Jetty
7 of 8
What it is
Structure extending into water
Primary Purpose
Usually built for wave protection; sometimes used for mooring or berthing smaller vessels.
🇪🇺
Quay
8 of 8
What it is
A waterfront structure similar to a wharf
Primary Purpose
Common term in Europe. Ships berth alongside it for loading and unloading cargo.
Port Terminology
Understanding Port Facilities
Port
An entire harbor or maritime facility
A place where ships arrive, load/unload cargo, and receive services.
Terminal
A specialized area within a port
Handles a specific type of cargo (containers, oil, LNG, passengers, etc.).
Berth
The exact location where a ship ties up
Assigns a ship to a loading/unloading position.
Wharf
A long platform alongside the shore
Ships moor directly alongside it.
Pier
A structure extending into the water
Ships can berth on one or both sides.
Dock
Can mean either a docking area or an enclosed basin
Often used generally as 'where ships dock.'
Jetty
Structure extending into water to protect the harbor or support mooring
Usually for wave protection; sometimes used for berthing.
Quay
A waterfront structure similar to a wharf
Common term in Europe.
