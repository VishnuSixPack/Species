    Is this really an aggregated harvest?

    You can report an aggregated harvest only when the catch came from small-scale vessels:

    • Each vessel is 12 m or less in length, or 20 GT or less
    • The catch went to a single collection point on the same calendar day

    You won't need individual vessel names. Instead you give the number of vessels and who ran the collection point.

    How SIMP works

    What is SIMP?

    The Seafood Import Monitoring Program is a U.S. traceability program run by NOAA Fisheries. For 13 species groups at high risk of illegal fishing or fraud, the importer must report where and how the fish was caught before it can enter U.S. commerce. Tuna is one of those groups.

    Which tunas are covered?

    Albacore (ALB), bigeye (BET), Atlantic bluefin (BFT), Pacific bluefin (PBF), southern bluefin (SBF), skipjack (SKJ) and yellowfin (YFT). A proposal to widen this to all tunas and tuna-like species was withdrawn, so these seven are the list.

    Who does what

    Importer of record holds a NOAA International Fisheries Trade Permit (IFTP), is responsible for the data, and keeps the chain-of-custody records.
    Customs broker usually keys the data into CBP's ACE system at entry.
    Exporter / supplier has the harvest and landing details the importer needs.

    Two kinds of data

    Reported at entry through ACE: IFTP, vessel name and flag, fishing authorisation, vessel ID, gear, species, catch area, landing date and port, who received the fish, and product form and weight at landing.
    Kept on file, and shown only if audited: the records tracing every handover from landing to U.S. entry.

    Other U.S. tuna programs (not covered here)

    Tuna Tracking and Verification Program: NOAA Form 370 for frozen and processed tuna, tied to dolphin-safe rules.
    HMS International Trade Permit: needed for bluefin, with ICCAT / CCSBT catch documents.
    A tuna shipment may need these as well as SIMP.

    What this tool does

    Helps you gather and check all the SIMP data for one entry, then save, print or export it for your filer. It does not file anything with CBP or NOAA.
