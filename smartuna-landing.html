// features

One record per order. Every shipment linked.

Type the master order once. Shipments, lines and documents inherit what they need, and stay connected to the catch behind them.

Master orders

One product, one external reference. Shipments are numbered from it: 34567CMC.1, .2, .3.

Shipment tracking

Route timeline, overdue ETAs and missing documents flagged before your buyer asks.

Excel import

Drop in your template. Containers, batches and duplicates are sorted before anything is saved.

Catch & species

FAD, FAD Free, longline or pole & line. Species, FAO area and certificates on every lot.

Mixed containers

Products sharing a customer order, container and seal are linked automatically.

Filtered exports

Pick a module, filter the rows, choose the columns. Keys are always included.

// how it works

From order to proof of origin in four steps.

Create the master order

Parties, product, packaging and ports, once.

Add or import shipments

By hand or from Excel. Details copy across.

Link the catch

Batches and raw materials tie each line to a vessel.

Share the trace

Export, report or show the full chain on demand.

// for developers

Your data, one query away.

Everything sits in Postgres. Read shipments, write notes and run imports with the same rules the app uses.

-- shipments still at sea
select reference, status, eta
  from smartuna.v_shipment_list
 where status = 'In transit'
 order by eta;
