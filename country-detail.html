What is a Flag of Convenience? A Flag of Convenience (FOC) occurs when a vessel is registered in a country different from the owner's nationality — often to benefit from lower taxes, cheaper fees, or lax enforcement of fishing and labour regulations. Regional bodies like the WCPFC now require rigorous tracking of the beneficial owner to prevent illegal fishing under FOC registrations.
