📋 Memorandum of Understanding
Data Privacy &
Confidentiality Agreement
Between Project Manhattan (Operator) and the Participating Company (You)
1. Purpose

This Memorandum of Understanding ("MoU") governs the terms under which your company voluntarily submits supply chain traceability data ("Assessment Data") to Project Manhattan for the purpose of generating a Manhattan Risk Index (MRI) score.

2. Data Collection

We collect only the information you voluntarily provide during this assessment. This includes product details, supply chain stage responses, Key Data Element (KDE) availability declarations, and evidence files you choose to upload.

3. Non-Disclosure

Project Manhattan agrees that your Assessment Data will not be shared, sold, or disclosed to any third party without your prior written consent. Your data is used solely for calculating and delivering your MRI score.

4. Data Retention

Evidence files are retained only for the duration of the review process. Upon your written request, all data will be permanently deleted within 72 hours.

5. Self-Declaration

All responses are self-declarations. Project Manhattan does not independently verify information unless separately engaged for a Manhattan Verified assessment.

6. Right to Withdraw

You may withdraw at any time before submission. Contact privacy@projectmanhattan.io to request deletion after submission.
