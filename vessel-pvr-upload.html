How it works

Matched by UVI numberEach row is matched to your registry using the UVI Number column, against the vessel's IMO or UVI field.

Only changes are writtenEach measure is compared field by field with the stored value. Unchanged vessels are skipped.

Unknown vessels are skippedRows that match no vessel are listed in the review but never created automatically.

Full audit trailEach change is recorded in the vessel timeline, and you can download the log before and after importing.
