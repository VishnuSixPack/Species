📘 EU Catch Support Form — Guide
What is EU CATCH?

CATCH is the European Commission's TRACES NT module for fisheries. It's how catch certificates and related documents get validated and tracked for seafood entering the EU market, replacing paper-based catch certificates with a digital system.

Why does this matter?

Under the EU IUU Regulation (EC) No 1005/2008, fishery products can't legally enter the EU without a valid Catch Certificate confirming the catch is legal, reported, and regulated. No valid CC generally means the shipment gets held or rejected at the border.

Key roles

Exporter — the business shipping the product out of its country of origin.
Importer — the business bringing the product into the EU.
Validating Authority — the flag state of the fishing vessel; whoever actually issued the original Catch Certificate (e.g. Department of Fisheries, Thailand).

The document types

Catch Certificate (CC) — the core document confirming legal catch.
Simplified Catch Certificate (SC) — a lighter version for small-scale vessels (under 12m without towed gear, or under 8m with towed gear).
Processing Statement (PS), Importer Declaration (ID), Storage Documents (SD) — related documents further down the chain. (Not yet built in this tool — coming soon.)

Direct Use vs No Direct Use

Direct Use — the Exporter has already submitted the CC and PS in CATCH. The Importer reuses that submission directly when launching the Importer Declaration. (EU-preferred path.)
No Direct Use — the Importer only has a PDF/paper CC from their supplier, and has to enter that data into the system themselves before launching the ID.

The overall submission flow
Catch Certificate (or Simplified CC) → Processing Statement → Importer Declaration → Submit for Import Control
What this tool does today

Helps you assemble and organize the data a Catch Certificate or Simplified Catch Certificate needs, using your own Vessel, Company, Port, and Country records — so you're not starting from a blank TRACES NT form. Processing Statement, Importer Declaration, and full EU submission are on the roadmap.

⚠️ Warning

You are about to proceed with the Simplified Catch Certificate, acknowledging that the vessel(s) involved meet at least one of the following conditions:

  • Overall length of less than 12 metres and does not use towed gear
  • Overall length of less than 8 metres and uses towed gear

Per Commission Regulation (EC) No 1010/2009, Art. 6(1), as amended.
