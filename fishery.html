MSC fisheries

Certified fisheries, their units of certification, vessels, ports and certificate history.
The Marine Stewardship Council (MSC) Fisheries program is a global organization with a certification standard and ecolabel that promotes sustainable fishing. It evaluates wild-capture fisheries based on sustainable fish stocks, minimal environmental impact, and effective management.
