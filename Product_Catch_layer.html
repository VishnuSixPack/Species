Identification Rules

Unique GTIN Automatically identify the MSC canned product.

Shared GTIN Ask which MSC canned product.

Packaging If that product comes in several case sizes (24 × 185g, 36 × 185g, 48 × 185g), ask which one.

Catch Type If it still isn't unique, ask the catch type: FAD, FAD Free, Longline or Pole & Line.

No Source GTIN Identify using the Internal ID and MSC canned product name.
